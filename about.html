About Us | ჩვენ შესახებ

Proxy Life started with a simple frustration: the cyber aesthetic we loved was everywhere online in games, in art, in music but nowhere on our shoulders. You could find the style in a million digital worlds, yet almost no store actually made bags and accessories that carried it into the real one. So we built it ourselves. We wanted the future to feel like something you hold, not just something you scroll past. Every piece we make is designed for people who see the world a little differently who like their gear to look like it belongs one timeline ahead. But we never wanted style to come at the cost of function. Our bags are built to carry your actual life: your laptop, your cables, your chaos just wrapped in a look that says you're already where everyone else is heading. That's the whole idea behind the name. A proxy is a stand-in, a version of something that comes before the real thing. Proxy Life is our stand-in for the life we're moving toward and we think you should be able to carry a piece of it today. Because right now, there's a gap. The aesthetic is popular, the demand is there, and yet almost nobody is making this style of bags and accessories properly. We're here to fill that gap one piece at a time.
